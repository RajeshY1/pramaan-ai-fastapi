# Product Requirements Document

## Product Name

Pramaan-AI

## Product Type

B2B SaaS Voice-Consent Platform

## Vision

Enable informed consent through multilingual voice interactions.

## Target Customers

### Primary

- FinTech
- Digital Lending
- Insurance

### Secondary

- Healthcare
- SaaS Platforms
- Government Services

## User Problem

Users do not understand legal consent documents.

This leads to:

- Drop-offs
- Compliance Risks
- Poor Customer Experience

## Solution

A Voice AI platform that explains consent in native languages and captures verbal consent.

## Success Metrics

- Consent Completion Rate
- User Understanding Score
- Onboarding Completion Rate
- Compliance Accuracy
- User Satisfaction

## MVP Scope

- Voice Input
- Voice Output
- Multilingual Support
- Consent Capture
- Consent Logging
