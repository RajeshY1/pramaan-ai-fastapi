# 🎙️ Pramaan-AI

## The B2B SaaS Voice-Consent Gateway for Vernacular India

Pramaan-AI is a multilingual Voice AI platform designed to transform compliance and consent workflows into natural voice conversations.

Instead of forcing users to read lengthy privacy policies and consent forms, Pramaan-AI explains data usage in the user's native language, answers questions in real time, and captures informed verbal consent.

---

## Problem Statement

Millions of users abandon onboarding journeys because they do not understand:

- Privacy Policies
- Consent Forms
- Data Sharing Agreements
- KYC Requirements
- Loan Documentation

Businesses face:

- Lower conversion rates
- Compliance risks
- User confusion
- Increased support costs

Pramaan-AI makes compliance conversational.

---

## Product Vision

Enable every user, regardless of language or literacy level, to understand and provide informed consent through natural voice conversations.

---

## Key Features

### Voice-to-Voice Consent

Natural voice conversations for consent capture.

### Multilingual Support

- Telugu
- Tamil
- Kannada
- Malayalam
- Hindi
- English

### Real-Time Q&A

Users can ask:

- Why do you need my Aadhaar?
- How will my data be used?
- Who can access my information?

### Consent Audit Trail

Captures:

- Consent Status
- Timestamp
- Language Used
- Interaction Summary

### Compliance Intelligence

DPDP-aware explanations and workflows.
